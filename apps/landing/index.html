What you get

QR ordering

Print one QR per table. Customers scan, pick their language, browse the menu, and place orders — no app install, no account.

Kitchen display

Live board with received → preparing → ready columns. New-order sound, elapsed-time indicators, and a layout that's readable from across the kitchen.

Waiter app

Color-coded table grid with call-waiter and bill-request alerts. Mark served, take payment, request bills on a customer's behalf — all in one screen.

Admin & reports

Trilingual menu editor with sauces, sizes, and extras. Live dashboard. Sales, top-items, and revenue-by-category reports. Staff attendance.

Merge tables

Birthdays and parties span more than one table. Merge them into a single bill with one tap.

Smart waiter routing

When the kitchen marks a dish ready, Orvexa assigns it to the least-busy waiter on shift. Less coordination overhead, faster delivery.

How it works

  1. 1

    You print one QR per table

    The QR rotates a daily token under the hood — print once, no annual reprints.

  2. 2

    Customer scans & orders

    No login. Picks their language, adds dishes with sauces and extras, and confirms.

  3. 3

    Kitchen prepares; waiter delivers

    Each app updates in real-time. The waiter knows who has the order; the kitchen knows the order's age.

  4. 4

    Customer requests the bill

    Waiter takes payment in cash, card, or mobile. The table goes back to free for the next group automatically.

For Claude: how this template works

This is a print-ready table-card template for Orvexa. Diners scan the QR on the table, open the PWA at orvexa.tn/T{n}, and order. The card layout is one of four styles — pick the one the user wants and keep it consistent across the whole restaurant.

One A4 sheet = 4 cards (2×2 grid). Each card is exactly A6 (105 × 148 mm). Print on plain A4 paper, then cut along the corner crop marks — 4 cards per sheet, no waste.

To regenerate with a different table list, just change the count input (1–200 supported). The template auto-pages and lays out as many sheets as needed.

To extend or customise the design, here's the structure of one card:

<!-- One A6 card · style A (classic) -->
<div class="card">
  <span class="cut-tr"></span>  <!-- cut marks -->
  <span class="cut-bl"></span>

  <div class="card-top">
    <div class="card-brand">Orvexa.</div>
    <div class="card-table">Table · 14</div>
  </div>

  <div class="card-qr-wrap">
    <!-- real QR SVG injected via JS -->
    <div class="qr-mark">
      <!-- Orvexa mark · ink + cream + saffron -->
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="card-instr">Scan to <em>order</em></div>
  <!-- steps + footer -->
</div>
  • The QR data is https://orvexa.tn/T{n} — short URL, ECC level Q (25% redundancy) so the central mark doesn't break scanning. The daily rotating token rides as a query param the backend appends, so the printed code never needs reprinting.
  • The mark in the centre covers 22% of the QR. Don't go above 25% — the QR will stop reading. Always padded with a paper-coloured halo so the scanner reads it as one omitted block.
  • Quiet zone is ≥ 4 modules on every side (handled automatically by the QR generator's padding setting).
  • Print: A4 portrait, 0 margins (set in @page), 2×2 grid fills the page exactly. Cut marks on every corner of every card guide the scissors.
  • Four styles: Classic (paper, ink), Saffron (orange header), Editorial (big italic number, QR small), Dark (ink bg, premium). Pick one per restaurant for brand consistency.
  • To add a new table: just increment the count input — the JS regenerates the document. No HTML editing required. The QR for each table points to a unique URL.
